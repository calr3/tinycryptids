# A Turing machine for Brocard's conjecture

**Current best: `machines/brocard_primes_72.txt`, a 72-state, 2-symbol Turing machine**
(named-state format, see `../tools/tm.py`). Started on a blank tape, it **halts iff
[Brocard's conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brocard's_conjecture) is false**: iff for
some consecutive primes `3 ≤ p < q` there are fewer than 4 primes between `p²` and `q²`.
Lean proves this (`TM.BrocardPrimes.M72.halts_iff`):

    Halts M72.delta2 M72.start2 ↔ ∃ p q, 3 ≤ p ∧ Prime p ∧ Prime q ∧ p < q ∧
      (∀ r, p < r → r < q → ¬ Prime r) ∧ primeCount (q ^ 2) - primeCount (p ^ 2) < 4

with `Prime` and `primeCount` (the number of primes `≤ x`) from
`../lean/TinyCryptids/Defs.lean`. `RECORDS.md` lists the machines, and `PROOF.md` walks
through the proof in plain words.

The 72-state machine is obtained in two stages:

1. `brocard_primes.txt` is the readable **108-state design**, generated by `build.py`. The Lean
   proof is written for this machine (`TM.BrocardPrimes.halts_iff`).
2. `../tools/minimize.py` merges states of the design that never disagree on a transition the
   run uses (an exact SAT search, `kissat404`). `gen_reduced_lean.py` carries the theorem over,
   and `machines/brocard_primes_72_annotated.txt` is the same machine with each state named after
   its roles in the design.

## How it works

It is `../legendre-sieve`'s incremental sieve, which decides `m = 2, 3, 4, …` in turn, with two
additions: a **marker** between the primes whose square has been passed and the others, and a
**counter** of the primes found since the last prime square. At checkpoint `m` the tape is

    M 00 P_k 00 … P_j 00 1 00 P_(j-1) 00 … P_1 000 S 0 1^(c+1)

* `M = 1^(m+2)` is a plain counter.
* `P_i`: one block per prime `p ≤ m`, largest first: a 1, a region of `p` cells with one
  **hole** (a single 0) at index `p − 1 − (m mod p)`, a 1. The hole moves one cell left per step
  and wraps from index 0 to index `p − 1` exactly when `p` divides the new `m`.
* The **marker** `00100` stands right of the block of `q`, the smallest prime with `q² > m`.
  The sweep and the home routine cross it like a separator. (For `m < 9` it stands right of the
  block of 3, so that 4 is not treated as a prime square: Brocard's statement starts at `p = 3`.)
* `S` is the square counter: a region of `2n + 1` cells (`n = isqrt m`), hole at index
  `2n − (m − n²)`. It wraps exactly when the new `m` is `(n + 1)²`.
* `1^(c+1)` is the **counter** K: `c` is the number of primes found since the last prime square
  `p²` (`p ≥ 3`; before 9 every prime counts, starting from 1 at `m = 2`).

One step `m → m + 1`:

1. **Home**: go left until four 0s, write a 1 there (`M` grows), turn round.
2. **Sweep** right over the P blocks, moving each hole (or wrapping it). A wrap means that prime
   divides `m + 1`; from then on the sweep runs in a second copy of its states
   ("composite mode"). The `000` ends it.
3. **Prime** (no block wrapped): move S's hole (it cannot wrap), append a 1 to K (`c += 1`), go
   home and **copy M** to its left: the old `M` (`p + 2` cells for the new prime `p = m + 1`)
   becomes the block of `p`, its hole at the last index.
4. **Composite**: move S's hole. If it wraps, `m + 1 = (n + 1)²`: S grows by 2 (K's first cell
   becomes S's guard, K moves 2 cells right), and a scan goes left to the marker. The block left
   of it is `q`'s. `q` divides `(n + 1)²` (its hole is at the last index) iff `q = n + 1`, since
   `q > n`: a **prime square**. Otherwise go home.
5. **Prime square**: `q`'s block moves 3 cells right (its left guard becomes the new marker;
   the hole is at the last index, so only four cells change). Then to the right end of the tape
   (four 0s) and back: K is erased from the right but for its first cell; if fewer than 5 cells
   are erased (`c < 4`), **halt**. Then go home.

So the machine halts at `q²` exactly when fewer than 4 primes were found since `p²`, `p` the
prime before `q`: `π(q²) − π(p²) < 4`.

State budget of the 108-state design: seed 24, home and grow 7, sweep 17 (7 per mode and 3 for a
wrap), S after a prime-mode sweep 8, S after a composite-mode sweep 11, the scan for the marker
7, the prime square 17, home and copy 17. The seed writes the tape for `m = 2`,
`1111 00100 1101 000 11011 0 11`.

Designs that were tried (all pass the tests; merged with `minimize.py`):

* This design: 108 states → 72 (`kissat404` and `cadical195`; the searches for 71 were stopped
  without a result).
* The same with a marker move that writes the cell after the block on the way back right
  (107 states): → 73.
* K in front of S as `1^a 0 1^c` (a hole separating old counts; S moves one cell right with its
  hole in place at a prime, K is checked from the `000` in front of it): 103 states → 74.
* S as an ordinary block of the sweep, `S 00 1^(c+1)` after it: 102 states → 76.
